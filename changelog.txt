Version: 2.3.0
Date: 2026-11-09
  Features:
    - Added frozen sprites for the distillery, electric furnaces, and all-corners storage tank.
    - Added water reflection sprites for solar panels.
    - Added fire sprites for the heat source.
    - Added technology icons for the air compressor and alien research.
    - Added base layers for the logistic chest icons to `graphics/icons/chest-logistic`.
    - Added `robot-tool-construction` icon.
  Changes:
    - Revised pipe connection artwork in the distillery base layer.
    - Updated the vehicle fission reactor equipment to use sprites matching the nuclear reactor appearance of vanilla; the original equipment sprites are available under `graphics/equipment/equipment-vehicle-fission-reactor-original`.
    - Updated the robot tool icons using the revised mask process.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed pixel misalignment in stone furnace remnant rotations.
    - Fixed that the fast/slow accumulator icons were swapped.
  Breaking Changes:
    - Renamed the burner generator and logistic zone interface icons to `graphics/icons/generator-burner.png` and `graphics/icons/robo-zone-interface.png`.
    - Renamed the lights layers of the boiler and oil boiler technology icons to `graphics/technology/boiler/boiler-lights.png` and `graphics/technology/boiler-oil/boiler-oil-lights.png`.
    - Moved the base layer of the area mining drill technology icon to `graphics/technology/mining-drill/mining-drill-area-base.png`, and renamed the base layer of the mining drill technology icon to `graphics/technology/mining-drill/mining-drill-standard-base.png`.
    - Moved the repair pack, water pumpjack, and nuclear power technology icons to Artisanal Reskins: Vanilla Assets.
    - Renamed the light blue module intermediate icons to `graphics/icons/module-intermediates/light-blue/light-blue-{level}.png`, and the smoke stack of the small burner assembling machine icon to `graphics/icons/assembling-machine-burner-small/assembling-machine-burner-small-smoke-stack.png`.
    - Renamed the pink alien artifact, small alien artifact, and alien science pack icons to `graphics/icons/alien-artifacts/alien-artifact-pink.png`, `graphics/icons/alien-artifacts-small/alien-artifact-small-pink.png`, and `graphics/icons/alien-science-packs/alien-science-pack-pink.png`.
    - Renamed the `robot-tool` icon to `robot-tool-logistics`.
    - Renamed and reorganized the fuel cell icons under `graphics/icons/fuel-cells`.
